# touch_grass
A mobile game where you identify real plants through daily challenges! Each day you get three target plants native to Finland. Find them outdoors, photograph them, and the app checks your photo with a plant-recognition API. Successful finds build your daily streak and raise your leaderboard position.

# How the game works
1. Register or log in.
2. Get your daily challenges. Every day you receive 3 random plants, each shown with a picture and its common name. Challenges reset at midnight.
3. Find the plant. Open the camera, take a photo of a plant, and send it for analysis. You can retake the photo if you are not happy with it.
4. Send the result for analysis. If the result matches one of today's targets, the challenge is marked complete. You have unlimited attempts.
5. Earn progress. Each newly completed challenge adds one recognition to your total.
6. Climb the leaderboard. Players are ranked by total recognitions.
